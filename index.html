Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Reset 2027.

How do I restore a purchase?
On the plan screen, tap "Restore purchases".

Why does Duo ask me to sign in to iCloud?
Duo stores your shared profile in iCloud, so the device needs an iCloud account.

How do I delete my Duo data?
In the Duo card, tap "Stop sharing" and confirm.
