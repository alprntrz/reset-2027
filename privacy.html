Reset 2027 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Reset 2027 ("the app") is published by Alperen Terzi ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data that stays on your device

Your answers to the onboarding questions, your first name, your habit plan and your daily progress are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Data shared when you use Duo

Duo is optional. Nothing is uploaded until you tap "Start a Duo". When you do, the app stores a profile in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database containing:

  • your first name, as you entered it
  • your OVR rating, streak, XP total and the number of habits you completed today
  • a random 6-character code that identifies the profile

Anyone who has your code can read this profile in the app. The profile is not linked to your Apple Account, email address or device in anything we can access. We use it only to show your progress to your Duo partner.

To delete this data, tap "Stop sharing" in the Duo card and confirm. The profile is removed from iCloud and your code stops working.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules one reminder per day on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. To show the right plans and check whether your subscription is active, the app uses Adapty. Adapty receives your purchase and subscription history and a random identifier it assigns to the app on your device. Collection of the advertising identifier (IDFA) and your IP address is turned off.

Usage analytics

To understand which parts of the app help people keep going, the app sends usage events to PostHog: for example, which onboarding step you reached, that a paywall was shown, or that a habit was checked off. Events carry a random identifier, counts and categories (such as the areas you picked). They never include your name, your habit titles or anything you type.

Crash and error reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry so we can fix it. Reports contain technical details such as the device model, iOS version and where the error happened. They do not include your name or progress.

What we do not do

The app contains no advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell your data. The services above process data only to provide their service to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
